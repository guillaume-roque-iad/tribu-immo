Becoming an advisor

Reconversion in real estate in 2026:
Where to start?

By Guillaume Roque • Manager Platinum iad France • Reading: 6 min •Published on 15 June 2026 • Updated: 14 August 2026

To be retained

  • A conversion in real estate requires three preliminary checks: cash, sector and actual availability.
  • The most common entry status is that ofindependent commercial agent registered in the SFCR.
  • No degree is required to act as an agent, but authorization and continuing training are mandatory.
  • No income, no delay, no volume of sales are guaranteed: the conversion is prepared financially before preparing commercially.

Changing the trade for real estate has never been more accessible than in 2026. With the fall in interest rates and a market that goes back, more and more assets seeking meaning and freedom choose to become real estate advisor. But successful conversion is being prepared. Here are the key steps to take the step calmly.

Why convert to real estate in 2026

A profession of freedom and human relationship

The real estate advisor organizes his time, chooses his area and builds his own business. It is a field, contact and advisory job, ideal for those who want to leave an overly rigid employee environment while keeping a true accompaniment.

Which pushes us to reconvert

The motivations most frequently expressed during the interviews are the autonomy of organization, the desire for a field and contact job, and the ability to match the effort provided with the result. However, there is no guarantee that a conversion will succeed: the activity remains to be built, without guaranteed income or time.

No real estate degree is required to join a network of agents: training and support make the difference.

The stages of successful conversion

1. Select the right status

The vast majority of advisors are independent commercial agents registered with the SFCR. This status, simple to create, allows you to start quickly, control your loads and change your activity at its own pace.

2. Forming before starting

Good initial training covers the legal framework, estimation, prospecting and negotiation. At iad, the training is ongoing, accessible online and in the field, and accompanied by an experienced sponsor who transmits the right reflexes.

3. Building on a network

Starting alone is possible, but starting around changes everything. A network brings branding, digital tools, dissemination on portals, legal support and above all a community that shares its methods.

How much does a conversion adviser earn?

The remuneration depends directly on the activity and the sales made. In a network of agents, the adviser receives a much higher share of commission than that of an employee, and can develop additional income by sponsoring new advisers in turn. The first months are used to build his portfolio: regularity takes precedence over precipitation.
