Real estate in Quarante (34310) :
buy, sell, invest in 2026

By Guillaume Roque • Representative iad France • Reading: 6 min •Published on 14 April 2026 • Updated: August 15, 2026

Quarante is a village in the northwest of the Hérault, between the plain and the first reliefs. Its real estate market is first of all in sales actually recorded: 86 houses sold between 2023 and 2025, a median of 114 000 €, and a considerable dispersion from one type of property to another.

Quarante (34310) — between vines, garrigues and abbey

Portrait of the village

Quarante is a character village located in the northwest of the Hérault, halfway between Béziers (30 min) and Saint-Chinian (15 min). With less than 2,000 inhabitants, its stone alleys, its 11th-century Romanesque abbey listed as a historic monument and its vineyards AOC Saint-Chinian, the village combines authenticity and quality of life.

The real estate market at Quarante

According to the property observed by Guillaume Roque on the area, the municipality is of interest to both purchasers of principal residence and secondary residence. Property with a garden or terrace is more likely to require a visit. The prices actually recorded in the municipality are detailed below, from official sales: what sales actually recorded say.

Quarante have all the essential services: primary school, local shops, doctor, party room. The municipality enjoys a very active associative life that promotes the integration of newcomers.

Why buy at Quarante

The town offers an exceptional living environment less than 30 minutes from Béziers and less than an hour from Montpellier. The natural setting — vineyards, olive groves, garrigues — seduces families and teleworkers who leave large cities. The price level is reflected in the sales recorded, detailed below.

Local market specifications

The majority of the property available is old village houses which sometimes require renovation. According to the properties observed by Guillaume Roque on the area, the renovated properties that meet current energy standards generate much more requests for visits than the unrenovated building. Properties with swimming pool or large plot are intended for a smaller audience but specifically for research purposes. This field observation does not constitute statistics, price guarantees or time limits.

What sales actually recorded say

Between 1ir January 2023 and 31 December 2025, 86 sales of houses were recorded at Quarante and retained after filtering. The median price is 114 000 €, for a median built-up surface of 90 m².

The figure that really describes this market is not the median, but the gap: the central half of sales is between 68 800 € and 214 800 €. The top of this fork is worth more than three times the bottom. In a village of this size, this means that there is not a market of Quarante, but at least two.

The land, the market-sharing line of the Quarantian market

A median plot of 122 m² on a village surrounded by vineyards is not an anomaly: it is the signature of a market that is played inside the village. The houses that are sold are mostly adjoining, developed in height, with at best a courtyard or terrace. Goods with real land exist, but they make up the top quarter of sales — the one that draws the range around €214,800.

The practical consequence is direct. A seller who compares his house with a garden to a communal median underestimates himself. A village house salesman who compares himself to a mas sale overestimates himself, and his property stays online. The comparable relevant to Quarante is not the municipality, it is the type of property.

Why this page does not give apartment prices

No sale of apartments was recorded at Quarante between 2023 and 2025. This is not a gap in the data: the village park is composed of houses. Any value per square metre presented elsewhere as "the price of apartments in Quarante" is, at best, an extrapolation from a nearby municipality.

Estimate a house at Quarante: four checks before talking price

  • The exact type of good. Townhouse with courtyard, pavilion with land, isolated farmhouse: four markets, four forks.
  • The outside, and its nature. A terrace of 12 m² on the roofs does not compare to a closed courtyard without view. In the ancient heart, this is the first criterion for sorting buyers.
  • The state of the roof and facades. On stone construction, these two posts are the ones that push back a buyer, because he does not know how to quantify them. Quotes attached to the file remove the argument from the negotiation.
  • Energy classification. Since 1stir January 2025, a G-class dwelling no longer meets the energy decency criterion and can no longer be subject to a new housing lease (Ministry of Ecological Transition). On old unrenovated buildings, this removes the share of investors buyers from the market.

Frequently Asked Questions on the Quarante-Year Market

Why do the prices announced for Quarante vary so much from one site to another?

Because most calculate an average of all categories, out of a number of sales that are too small to be stable. With around 30 sales per year and a range ranging from €68,800 to €214,800, two atypical sales are enough to move an annual figure of several tens of thousands of euros. This is why this page publishes a median of three years and refuses to publish one per year.

Is a village house without exterior sold in Quarante?

It sells, but to a narrower audience and at a different pace. The absence of an outsider is not a resiliency for a purchaser who is looking for a foot-to-earth or a secondary residence with a budget content; it is for a family. The mistake is to position this property at the cost of a house with courtyard.

Do we need to renovate before we sell to Quarante?

Rarely entirely. What changes the perception of an ancient good is the lifting of uncertainties: a roof diagnosis, a electricity estimate, a situation of sanitation. A complete renovation committed to sell rarely recovers fully; a documented file, almost always.

You sell a house to Quarante?

At Quarante, everything is played on the right comparable: village house, house with courtyard or with land are not in the same fork. The estimate is free of charge, made on site, and based on sales from the same segment. It does not constitute a guarantee of sales price or a guarantee of time.
