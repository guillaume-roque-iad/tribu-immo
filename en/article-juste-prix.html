Selling quickly without brader:
the fair price method

By Guillaume Roque • Manager Platinum iad France • Reading: 6 min •Published on 10 July 2026 • Updated: 14 August 2026

All sellers share the same dream: to sell quickly, and at the best price. The good news is that these two objectives are not opposed. In 2026, with a market that goes back and buyers again, it is the positioning of the price that makes all the difference. Here's the right price method: the one that allows you to sell quickly, without ever stalling.

Why the fair price changes everything

The first weeks are decisive

A property generates the most interest in the first three to four weeks after it is posted online. It is at this point that the most motivated buyers, those who have been following the market for months, discover this. A well-positioned price from the start captures this wave of attention. A price too high lets it pass.

The price trap too high

Starting "high to keep the margin" is the most common error. The property receives few visits, settles in for the duration, and ends up worrying buyers who wonder why it does not sell. As a result, the successive declines give the image of a "sluggish" asset, and the sale often ends below what an initial fair price would have been.

A properly valued good sells on average much faster than an overvalued good — and often at a higher final price, as it attracts several buyers at the same time.

How to set the right price

1. Relying on actual sales

The fair price is not guessed: it is built from comparable goods actually sold in the sector, not from competitive prices. The prices charged reflect hopes; the selling prices reflect the market. A local councillor has these specific references, street by street.

2. Taking into account assets and defects

Two neighbouring properties may have very different values depending on exposure, condition, EPD, outdoor presence or parking. A serious estimate adjusts the price to these concrete criteria, rather than applying a simple price to the average m².

3. Stay lucid on the market of 2026

The market has recovered, but buyers remain informed and compare. They know prices, have online tools and negotiate with method. A good displayed at the right price inspires confidence and triggers action; an overvalued asset instead invites downward negotiation.

Just price doesn't mean low price

Selling at the right price is not underselling. It is to show the real value of the property, the one that the market is ready to pay today. As a guide, a good positioned at the right price can create a dynamic of several visits in a short time, sometimes several offers — which, far from lowering the price, secures it or even increases it. The rarity of a well presented and well positioned property plays in favour of the seller.

The role of the adviser

Beyond the estimate, the advisor builds a strategy: enhancement of the property, careful photos, wide dissemination on the portals, selection of buyers actually solvent and conduct of the negotiation. It is this accompaniment that turns a fair price into a successful, serene and fast sale.

Where'd you get those numbers? The price ranges shown on this page are shown in terrain markers identified by Guillaume Roque, independent real estate advisor iad, on the assets he has followed and observed, arrested as at 12 August 2026 from the properties observed in the area. These are neither certified statistical averages nor values of expertise. Reference data are public and verifiable: the database DVF (requests for land values) the actual transactions recorded by the tax administration, and theINSEE publishes data on the housing stock. The price of a specific property depends on its condition, its exposure, its exteriors, its floor and its exact location: only a visit makes it possible to establish it.
