The job in everyday life

A real estate advisor accompanies owners and buyers at each stage of a transaction: discovery of the project, estimation, taking of mandate, marketing, visits, negotiation, and then followed until the signature at the notary.

Prospecting and taking of mandates

It is the heart of the activity, and the most often underestimated part. The first few weeks do not consist of advertising: they are used to understand the sector, meet with owners, make themselves known and obtain the first mandates. Without a warrant, there's nothing to sell — no income.

Support for sellers and purchasers

To estimate with methodology, explain a market price to a seller who hoped more, qualify buyers, organize useful visits, carry out negotiations until the agreement, gather the diagnoses and secure the file until the compromise: it is a job of advice and mediation as well as of sale.

Organize an independent activity

You're a freelancer. This means choosing your schedules, but also managing your time, administrative procedures, cash flow and regularity. There is no fixed wage, guaranteed income, or hourly supervision: the structure is you who create it.

Status, costs and revenues: what to anticipate

A real estate advisor iad exercises as independent commercial agent, under cover of his principal's professional card. Here are the concrete points to know before you commit.

Registration

You must be registered in the Special Register of Commercial Agents (SRAC). Since 1stir January 2023, the application is made online on the Business formalities window, and no longer directly with the transplant. The formality must be carried out within 15 days of the start of the activity; its published cost is 23,21 € for an individual enterprise or micro-enterprise.

The clearance certificate

You do not hold the professional card: you exercise under cover of that of your principal, materialized by a certificate of authorisation the Chamber of Commerce and Industry. It is requested by the cardholder, and its expiry date cannot exceed that of the card.

Insurance and incompatibilities

One professional liability insurance is compulsory. The Hoguet Act also strictly regulates the activity: you cannot receive or hold funds, nor give legal advice, nor direct an institution or agency.

Continuing training

Continuing training is fixed at 14 hours a year, or 42 hours over three consecutive years Exercise. It is this second formulation that is generally used at the time of renewal of the authorization.

Fees and remuneration

The activity involves a monthly subscription and regular professional fees (insurance, travel, tools, communication). In return, the share of the fees paid to the adviser is significantly higher than that of an agency employee, with increments ranging from 69 % to 85 % of the fees according to annual turnover, under the applicable iad contractual conditions.

Income depends on transactions actually made and received. Their level and regularity vary according to the sector, business activity, experience and time to sell. No amount is guaranteed, and a transaction is paid only after it has actually been carried out — hence the importance of cash flow before you start.

This job is probably not for you if...

Let's just say it frankly: it's better to keep it away now than to find out in six months.

The project is likely to go wrong if:
  • you need a fixed income from the first month;
  • you have no financial reserve to cover the start-up period;
  • the prospecting throws you away and you rely solely on the ads to be contacted;
  • you can't leave regular time on your area;
  • you expect a framework, schedules and hierarchy that organize your day;
  • you want to develop a team without investing in real estate activity — the remuneration comes from transactions made, not memberships.

On the other hand, the project deserves to be studied if you are ready to learn a regulated profession, develop local relationships, regularly prospect, work with measurable goals and accept variable remuneration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you need a degree or a real estate experience?

No real estate degree is required to practise as a licensed commercial agent. Business, relational or entrepreneurial skills are useful, but training and implementation remain essential.

Can we start in parallel with another activity?

This depends on your employment contract, your actual availability and the rules applicable to your situation. These dots must be checked before starting, not after.

How long before you get income?

There is no guaranteed time limit. A transaction consists of several stages — mandate, offer, compromise, financing, act — and is paid only after it has been completed. Therefore, a transitional cash flow must be provided that is appropriate to your situation.

What administrative steps should be taken?

Registration in the Special Register of Commercial Agents is done online on the Business Formalities Window, within 15 days of the start of the activity, for a published cost of € 23,21. In addition, the certificate of authorization issued through your principal and a professional civil liability insurance.

Can an iad advisor hold his or her own professional card?

No. The commercial agent exercises under cover of his principal's card. Holding your own card means changing your status and meeting the requirements of the Hoguet law — professional fitness, insurance, financial guarantee where applicable.
