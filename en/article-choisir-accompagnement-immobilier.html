How do you choose effective mentoring as an independent property adviser?

Guillaume Roque · 12 octobre 2026

Before joining or changing property networks, assess mentoring through practical evidence: availability, training, case reviews and respect for your independence.

Check real availability

Ask how often you can meet one to one and what response time is realistic for case questions. Regular discussions beat vague promises of constant availability.

Request practical examples

Ask how a mentor helps prepare a valuation, review a seller meeting, improve follow-ups and analyse a setback. Actual situations reveal the working method.

Link training to practice

iad France describes online and in-person training alongside an initial support programme offered by a sponsor. Ask which practical exercises accompany the courses.

Keep your independence

Support should reflect your area, available time and individual priorities. It should not impose a single model or promise commercial results.

Six questions before choosing

When can we meet? Can I join a team meeting? How will we prepare early appointments? Which courses are available? How do we discuss setbacks? What is a realistic weekly plan?

How do you measure progress?

After a month, check whether your priorities are clearer, you have improved a skill and know where to ask for help. Mentoring supports learning but guarantees neither sales nor income.
