Introduction

Selling real estate is often one of the most important transactions in your life. This guide gives you all the keys to prepare, value and conclude the sale of your property at the best price, as soon as possible.

The 6 steps of a successful sale

1
Estimate your property correctly

Estimation is the key. An overvalued asset remains on the market and is depreciating. An undervalued asset makes you lose money. Criteria: surface, condition, exposure, floor, loads, proximity to services, comparable prices sold recently.

2
Preparing for your good

Home Staging lightweight, small repairs (robinery, paints, luminaires), complete cleaning, clean exterior. A few hundred euros invested can make several thousand euros out of the final prize.

3
Professional photos

95% of buyers discover a property online before visiting. Take photographs during the day with natural light, a wide-angle lens and all lights on. Carefully prepared photos multiply the number of enquiries by 3.

4
Collecting mandatory documents

DPE (mandatory), asbestos diagnosis (before 1997), lead diagnosis (before 1949), ERNT, Carrez (co-ownership). Your iad advisor helps you build up the complete file.

5
Manage visits

Be available on weekdays and weekends. Aerial before each visit. Let the buyer imagine himself at home. Answer questions honestly. Follow interested people quickly.

6
Negotiate and sign

Your iad advisor helps you evaluate offers and negotiate the best conditions. Compromise → 10 days of withdrawal → financing (45 days) → authentic act at the notary.

How long does a sale actually take?

A sale takes place in two stages that should not be confused:

PhaseWhat's on it?What lengthens it
MarketingFrom online to accepted offerA price too high, photos that don't show good, an incomplete file
ImplementationFrom compromise to authentic actTime to obtain the loan, purging of pre-emption rights, waiting for co-ownership documents

The first phase depends heavily on you. The second is much less: it follows an administrative and banking calendar on which no one has their hands.

The most expensive mistakes

  • Starting from a "to see" prize, even if it goes down afterwards. An asset that drags down is devalued in the minds of buyers, who follow the advertisements much longer than we can imagine.
  • Waiting for compromise to order diagnostics. An unfavourable diagnosis discovered late reopens negotiations at the worst time.
  • Hide a defect. It appears from the visit, or later, with a legal risk. It's better to announce and encrypt.
  • Multiply simple mandates thinking of expanding the distribution: the property then appears at different prices on the same portals, which worries the buyer.
  • Refusal to visit returns. Three buyers who make the same point say something useful.

Preparing for the good before the visits

Without work and without significant expenditure, three actions change perception:

  1. Unload : the purchaser must see the volume, not the business. It's the best-performing action.
  2. Repair small visible defects — handle that detachs, blackened joint, light bulb dead. Everyone is minor; their accumulation suggests a well-maintained well.
  3. Care for the light : open shutters, clear curtains, visits set on the brightest hours.

Photos matter because they trigger the visit — but they never sell to themselves. They must look like good, failing which the visit ends in a disappointment that closes the negotiations.
