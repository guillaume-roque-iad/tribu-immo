How much does an iad real estate agent earn? in 2026?

By Guillaume Roque • Independent Real Estate Advisor iad · manager • Reading: 7 min •Published on 13 April 2026 • Updated: 14 August 2026

To be retained

  • The remuneration of an iad advisor is Only variable : a share of the fees paid on sales signed by the notary.
  • It is moving forward by annual turnover levels, defined by the current iad contract conditions — not reproduced here to avoid outdated information.
  • The Management committees They depend on the sales actually received by the organisation's advisers; they involve effective support work.
  • No revenue, sales frequency and success time are guaranteed.

The issue of income is naturally the first issue for people who plan to join iad France. The answer is more complex than just a monthly salary, as iad advisors have several additional sources of income.

Source 1: Sales and Rental Commissions

This is the main income of an iad advisor: a share of the duty-free fees collected on each transaction actually signed with the notary. The percentage increases by annual turnover level:

  • From 0 to 64 999 € CA : 69 % honoraria
  • Between €65 000 and €80 000 of CA: 75 %, after deduction of administrative costs of € 190 tax excl. per sale in this period
  • Between 80 001 and 100 000 € CA: 80 %, with administrative costs of 240 € tax excl. per sale
  • From 100 001 € CA : 85 %, with administrative costs of 290 € tax excl. per sale

iad contract terms and conditions in force, verified by Guillaume Roque in August 2026. These conditions can change: only the iad contract documentation submitted during integration is authentic.

Example calculation. For a sale at €250 000 with a fee of 3.5% (€8,750 tax excl.), a consultant at the level 69% collects €6,037; at the level 85%, €7,437 for the same transaction. The actual amount then depends on the rate of fees negotiated for the mandate, the level reached in the year, the applicable administrative costs and any fee sharing with another advisor.

Which makes a counsellor's income vary

No average income figures are published on this site: differences between advisors are too large for an average to make sense, and a figure presented as "average" would be misleading. The factors that really weigh:

  • The volume and quality of mandates obtainedthe intensity and regularity of prospecting.
  • The average price of goods in the sector At the same fee rate, a market of 150 000 € and a market of 400 000 € do not produce the same amounts.
  • The transformation rate between returned warrants and signed deeds — sales that fail to finance do not earn anything.
  • Real Time the activity, especially in the first months.
  • The time limit for cashing : the remuneration follows the authentic act, several months after the mandate.

The time before a first transaction and the level of revenue vary greatly depending on the sector, time spent, prospecting, mandates obtained, funding deadlines and transactions actually made. No revenue, sales frequency and success time are guaranteed.

Source 2: Management committees (recurrences)

It is the most powerful specificity of the iad model. By sponsoring and training other advisors, you perceive commissions on their activities, on 5 levels:

  • Level 1 - your direct godsends: 7% to 14% fees, depending on your level and applicable devices
  • Level 2 - the godson of your godson: 3 %
  • Level 3 : 1,5 %
  • Level 4 : 0.5% to 1%
  • Level 5 : 0,2 % to 1 %

iad contract terms and conditions in force, verified by Guillaume Roque in August 2026. These conditions can change: only the iad contract documentation submitted during integration is authentic.

These managerial commissions depend on the sales actually made and collected by the advisors of your organization. They are variable, imply real accompanying work and do not constitute an automatic income or a guaranteed income. They can be an additional, variable and non-guaranteed source of revenue: their level depends entirely on the sales actually made by the organization's advisors.

Source 3: Arrangements for association with capital

The iad Group may propose, subject to conditions, certain arrangements for association with capital. Their access, terms and possible performance are neither automatic nor guaranteed. Only the official documentation in force is authentic.

How long to make a living with iad?

The lead time before a first transaction is not predictable and varies greatly from advisor to advisor. It depends in particular:

  • Your geographic sector and the dynamics of the local market
  • Your personal and professional network of departures
  • The time you spend on your activities
  • The quality of your manager's support
  • Your ability to develop a team quickly

Conseil de Guillaume Roque : The fastest successful advisors are those who combine sales and development activities in the first few months. The development of a team is an activity in its own right: it asks to develop its team, to train the advisers and to accompany them for the duration, and it does not exempt itself from producing.
