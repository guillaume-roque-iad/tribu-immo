Real estate in Sète et le bassin de Thau
in 2026

By Guillaume Roque • Manager Platinum iad France • Reading: 6 min •Published on 7 July 2026 • Updated: 14 August 2026

Between sea and pond, Sète and the Thau Basin form one of the most endearing territories of the Hérault. In 2026, this coastline, which combines maritime authenticity, art de vivre and proximity to Montpellier, continues to attract permanent residents, telework workers and buyers of secondary residence. Here are the major market trends and our tips for selling or buying at the right price.

A sought after territory between sea and pond

Sète, the singular

A singular island dear to Georges Brassens and Paul Valéry, Sète seduces by its canals, port, joustes and its preserved Mediterranean atmosphere. The railway service to Montpellier and Béziers places the city within reach of a wide employment pool, which broadens the public's interest. The property with sea views, canals or close to Mont Saint-Clair retains a lasting attraction.

A dynamic thau basin

Around the pond, municipalities like Mèze, Marseillan, Bouzigues, Balaruc-les-Bains or Frontignan complete the offer. Each has its own personality: oyster villages, spas, marinas. This mesh allows you to find both a holiday apartment and a family home just minutes from the sea.

The Thau Basin combines three markets in one: principal residence, secondary residence and seasonal rental investment. Identifying your target is the key to a successful sale.

Guide prices in 2026

Prices vary greatly depending on the municipality, location and view. In Sète, apartments are usually traded around 3,500 to 4,500 € per square metre, with far higher points for exceptional goods facing the sea. In the communes of the pond such as Mèze or Marseillan, values are usually found around €3,000 to €3,800 per square metre, while the villages more in retreat often remain more accessible.

These ranges are purely indicative: two neighbouring properties may show significant deviations depending on the state, exposure, parking or presence of an outsider. Only a personalized estimate, based on recent comparable sales, allows a fair price to be set.

On a popular coastline, the right price is played at the close detail: terrace, parking space, absence of opposites or immediate proximity to the water make all the difference.

Sell or buy at the right time

For sellers

According to the properties observed by Guillaume Roque on the area, the search for a secondary residence is more sustained in the beautiful season. However, this does not change the essence: a price consistent with the characteristics of the good and comparable sales makes it easier to obtain requests for visits, regardless of the period, while a price well above the market makes them scarce. This field observation does not constitute statistics, price guarantees or time limits.

For purchasers

The level of borrowing rates directly conditions the budgets of the purchasers: have your financing capacity established before visiting, it determines what is really accessible to you.

The exact perimeter of this page

Sète and the communes of the surroundings of the pond of Thau, in the Hérault.

  • Unique island: center, canals, high area
  • Mont Saint-Clair, villas area with views
  • Corniche and beaches, seafront
  • Communes of the pond : Balaruc, Bouzigues, Mèze, Marseillan

What goods are actually found there?

  • City apartments on the canals
  • Villas in the hill on Mont Saint-Clair
  • Beach residences of the cornice
  • Fishermen's houses and shellfish built around the pond

The setois market is one of the most segmented in the region: sight, exposure and access to parking create considerable differences between identical surface goods. The pressure of secondary residence and seasonal rental is high.

What makes the value here vary

These factors are sector specific: they do not translate from one municipality to another.

  • The view — sea, pond or canal — and its perennial character
  • Parking, rare and decisive on the island
  • Exposure to wind and salty moisture on the seafront
  • The rules applicable to short-term rental
  • The railway service to Montpellier

Frequently asked questions about Sète and the basin of Thau

Does the sea view justify a significant price difference?

Yes, it is the first factor in the development of the sector — provided it is clear and sustainable. A view that can be masked by future construction is much less valued.

Does the market of the pond of Thau behave like that of Sète?

The pond's municipalities form a separate, more residential market with buyers often in principal residence. Price logics and deadlines differ from those on the island.
