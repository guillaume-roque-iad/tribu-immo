Property advisers: which indicators should you review each week?

Guillaume Roque · Published on 8 November 2026

To manage your activity, track meaningful conversations, meetings held, progress on cases and next actions. A simple dashboard helps you decide what to improve; it cannot predict a commission or a sale date.

Separate activity, progress and results

A busy day does not necessarily move a project forward. Separate what you do, what it produces and what remains to be done. Count meetings actually held rather than only those booked. A meaningful conversation clarifies a need, a timeline or an agreed next step; an unanswered message belongs in a different category.

Choose five practical measures

You could track qualified conversations, meetings held, cases with a dated next action, viewing feedback received and time spent improving a skill. These five measures are an organisational suggestion, not a network standard. Set goals that reflect your availability, experience and area.

Understand gaps before increasing volume

Fictional example: several conversations lead to few meetings. Review how clearly you explain your service and qualify projects. If meetings take place but cases stall, examine missing information and follow-up commitments. Do not judge performance from a single week: compare several periods, allowing for holidays, project maturity and the time actually available.

End the review with a decision

Arrange a short weekly discussion with your mentor. Bring a concrete example for each difficulty, then choose an improvement you can test: prepare a better question, confirm a deadline or clarify a written update. Record the next action, its owner and its date. The following week, check what changed. A dashboard connects indicators with decisions; Bpifrance Création describes this management approach.

Frequently asked questions

How many contacts should I make?

No universal quota guarantees a sale. Start at a realistic pace and assess the quality of your conversations.

Is a spreadsheet enough?

Yes, if it stays up to date. A more complex tool only helps if it makes follow-up easier.

Should I share my entire contact list with my mentor?

Anonymised situations are often enough to work on your method. Share only necessary information through suitable tools.
