Real estate prices in Narbonne, Carcassonne and the Hauts Cantons :
medians DVF 2023-2025

By Guillaume Roque •Independent Real Estate Advisor iad •Published on 14 August 2026 •Updated: August 15, 2026

Key points

The figures below are calculated from land value requests (DVF), the basis of sales actually recorded at the notary. They cover the last three full years, from 1 January to 31 December.ir January 2023 to 31 December 2025.

  • A house sold in Narbonne was sold at a median price of 228 000 €, out of 1,097 sales retained. An apartment: 100 000 €, out of 2,032 sales.
  • In Carcassonne, the median is 148 200 € for a house and 67 500 € for an apartment. In Lézignan-Corbières, 150 000 € and 77 350 €.
  • In the villages of the Upper Townships, the medians are much lower and are based on few sales: 127 000 € in Saint-Chinian (89 sales), 114 000 € Quarante (86 sales), 59 894 € in Saint-Pons-de-Thomières (74 sales).
  • Houses and apartments are counted separately. Mixing them would produce a figure that does not correspond to any real property.
  • No statistics are published below 30 sales. Three out of twelve series remain empty: the apartment market in the three villages.
  • A median describes a set of sales, not a dwelling. The difference between two properties of the same surface area in the same municipality may be considerable, and DVF does not contain the state, the exposure, the exterior, or the energy performance.

The column "Central half of sales" shows the range of 50% of transactions, with a quarter remaining below and a quarter above. At Quarante, this interval ranges from €68,800 to €214,800 for a house: it is this dispersion, not the median alone, that really describes the market of a village.

Why the price per m² of a house should be read with caution

DVF records the built surface, never the value of the land separately. The price per square metre of a house therefore returns the total price — including land — to the living area alone. Two identical houses, one on 100 m² of land and the other on 2,000 m², will display a price per m² very different without the building being there for anything. Therefore, the median field surface is shown in the table. For an apartment, the indicator does not pose this problem.

How these figures were calculated

A DVF line is not a sale. The same transaction produces as many lines as lots and parcels concerned, and the price is repeated identically on each. Adding the lines would mean counting the same price several times — this is the most frequent error in the numbers found online. The lines were therefore grouped by mutation identifier.

Of 20,273 lines read and 8,712 mutations examined, 6,148 were retained. This is what has been ruled out, and why:

The indicator published is the Median and not the average: some exceptional sales move an average, not a median. The price per square metre is the median of the price/area ratios calculated for sale, not the ratio of two medians.

A limit remains, and it is reported rather than masked: a sale of parcels located in several communes appears only partially in the file of a commune. These cases are rare and are not detectable from an isolated communal file.

How to read this data without mistake

  1. Registered price and displayed price are not the same. DVF gives the price recorded for the act. Advertising portals display prices that are often higher than they are.
  2. Prefer the median to the average. The average is distorted by some atypical transactions; the median better describes the current market.
  3. Separate houses and apartments. Mixing them produces a figure that does not correspond to any real property.
  4. Look at the number of sales. In a village, there are a few annual transactions that do not allow any statistics. This is the case in most of the municipalities of the Minervois.
  5. Check the period. A current year is still incomplete: comparing it to a full year shows a decline that does not exist.
  6. Take into account the time frame for publication. DVF appears with several months of lag: the most recent data are not yet visible.

What a median never says. Two houses with the same surface area, on the same street, can be sold at very different prices depending on the state, the exposure, the calm, the view, the exteriors, the parking and the energy performance. None of these features are included in DVF. A midwayer locates a sector; it never replaces the examination of a specific good.

The municipalities covered and those not covered

Six communes are treated because they reach a volume of sales that allows a calculation: Narbonne, Carcassonne, Lézignan-Corbières, Quarante, Saint-Chinian and Saint-Pons-de-Thomières.

  • Narbonne It alone accounts for 3,129 of the 6,148 sales held, two thirds of which are apartments: it is the only municipality in the area where the apartment market is dominant.
  • Carcassonne and Lézignan-Corbières have a different balance, with a median house one third lower than that of Narbonne.
  • Quarante, Saint-Chinian and Saint-Pons-de-Thomières are village markets: between 74 and 89 house sales in three years, or a maximum of 30 per year. Their median is published only in the full period.

The other municipalities of the Minervois and the Hauts Cantons are not included in this table, and this is voluntary: their annual volume is counted in a few units. A median calculated on three sales is not a statistic, it is an accident. For these municipalities, the explorer DVF of Etalab allows you to view transactions one by one, which remains the most honest reading.
