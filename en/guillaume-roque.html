Accompanying method

For sellers

  1. Visit and record of the property : surface, condition, exposure, exterior, constraints.
  2. Argued estimate based on truly comparable sales of the sector, presented with its methodology and limitations.
  3. Positioning : a price that triggers visits in the first few weeks, rather than a poster price followed by successive decreases.
  4. Dissemination and monitoring : regular reporting, returns of visits transmitted as is, documented adjustment if necessary.
  5. Negotiation and follow-up until the act, including monitoring of the financing of the purchaser.

For the organization's advisors

  1. Project Cadming before any decision: cash, sector, actual availability.
  2. Start-up support — see the first 90 days of a real estate advisor iad.
  3. Regular follow-up points and work on pre-sale indicators.
  4. Continuing training and field support on difficult estimates and negotiations.
