To be retained

  • The funding prepares before visits Without established borrowing capacity, an offer has little weight.
  • The debt ratio is capped at 35 % Income, including insurance (recommendation of the High Financial Stability Board).
  • The purchase price is not the total cost: notary fees, warranty, works, charges and property tax are added.
  • The visit is not enough : the condominium file and urban planning often say the essential.

Introduction

Buying real estate is a major step in a life. Between project definition, research, funding and signature, the journey may seem complex. This guide will accompany you step by step to make your purchase in the best conditions.

The 6 steps of your real estate purchase

1
Set your project

Principal residence, secondary or rental investment? Apartment or house? Nine or old? Minimum surface area, number of rooms, priority geographic area, maximum budget and available input.

2
Assessing your borrowing capacity

Generally, your monthly payments should not exceed 35% of your net income. Example: for 3 000€/month of net income, monthly payments max = 1 050€, i.e. approximately 180 000€ borrowingable over 20 years at 3.5%.

3
Search for the ideal good

With your iad advisor: access to all ads (SeLoger, LeBonCoin, Logic-Immo) + exclusive iad portfolio. Set up alerts, visit quickly, prepare your questions before each visit.

4
Make an offer and negotiate

Your iad advisor helps you formulate a strategic offer. A written offer with agreement of banking principle shows your seriousness and accelerates the seller's decision.

5
Sign the sales compromise

The compromise binds both parties. 10 days of withdrawal. Security deposit 5 to 10%. Funding deadline: usually 45 days.

6
Authentic act and key delivery

Signature at the notary's. Payment of funds to the seller. Handing over the keys. You're officially the owner of your property!

Tip: Prepare your funding package before you start visiting. A buyer with a bank agreement in principle is much more credible and can negotiate better terms with the seller.

What to check before making an offer

Some of the bad surprises are read in documents, not during the visit:

  • In condominiums The last three minutes of general meeting, the work voted and not yet called, the actual amount of expenses, the status of unpaid expenses.
  • Home : the conformity of the sanitation, the existence of authorisations for extensions and verandas, the nature of the land.
  • In all cases : the state of the risks, the communal planning document — which indicates what can be built around — and any easements.

Make an offer that has a chance to succeed

  1. Make the offer in writing, with price, validity period and suspensive conditions.
  2. Attach proof of seriousness : banking simulation, agreement in principle, proof of contribution. That's what sets two close offers.
  3. Arguing a Lower Offer by objective elements — quantitative work, default found — rather than by the principle of negotiation.
  4. Give yourself a limit in advance and stick to it: the overbidding is decided cold, not in the emotion of a visit.

The 10 days withdrawal period After signing the compromise benefits the purchaser: it runs from the notification and allows to give up without justification or penalty.
