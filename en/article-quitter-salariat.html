Leave the salariate for real estate:
the typical route

By Guillaume Roque • Manager Platinum iad France • Reading: 6 min •Published on 30 June 2026 • Updated: 14 August 2026

You've been thinking about it for months. The wage earner has brought you stability, but the desire for freedom, deplorable income and a more humane profession takes over. In 2026, taking the step towards real estate is a decision that is preparing rather than improvising itself. Here is the typical course of a person who leaves an employed position to become an independent real estate advisor, step by step, without a blind spot.

Before you resign: lay the foundations

Clarify motivation

Leaving a stable job makes sense only if the project is solid. Advisors who succeed in their transition often share the same drivers: the taste for contact, the desire for autonomy and the acceptance of earnings related to results. Above all, it is a matter of turning frustration ("I no longer support my current framework") into a real ambition ("I want to build my own activity").

Assessing its financial situation

The golden rule: don't quit on a head start. The first months of activity are used to build a portfolio of mandates before sales materialize. It is therefore prudent to provide for a security cash flow that ideally covers several months of current expenses, as long as the first commissions arrive.

Tip: List your monthly fixed charges and multiply them by the number of months you think are necessary for your first sales. It's your starting mattress.

Choose the right exit door

Not all departures are worth it. Depending on your contract and situation, several devices can secure the transition. This information is indicative: the exact conditions must always be checked with France Travail (ex-Pôle employé) or a dedicated advisor, as they evolve regularly.

  • The conventional rupture - negotiated with the employer, it can open up the right to unemployment benefits while starting its activity.
  • The resignation for conversion Under conditions, a real and serious professional project can be recognized and give access to accompaniment.
  • Partial maintenance of allowances at the beginning of independent activity, in some cases time to increase.

The idea is not to bet everything on aid, but to know the levers available to leave in the best conditions.

The first weeks of counseling

Create Status

The vast majority of advisors start as an independent commercial agent, registered with the SFCR. This status is quick to create, readable on the load side and perfectly suited for a progressive launch. It is the formality that transforms the project into reality.

Forming and Surrounding

We don't leave the wage-earner to be alone. A solid initial training (legal framework, estimation, prospecting, negotiation) coupled with an experienced sponsor avoids beginner errors. At iad, the support is continuous, accessible online and in the field, and led by a community of counsellors who share their methods.

Building your business

The first months reward regularity, not haste. Daily prospecting, first mandates, first estimates: each action builds the portfolio that will generate recurring revenues tomorrow. Remuneration depends directly on the activity, with a much higher share of commission than that of an employee, and the possibility of developing additional income by sponsoring in turn.
