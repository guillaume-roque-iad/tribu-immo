Agent iad vs Real Estate Agent: the real differences

By Guillaume Roque • Independent Real Estate Advisor iad · manager • Reading: 7 min •Published on 13 April 2026 • Updated: 14 August 2026

To be retained

  • The real estate agent in an agency owns or depends on a business card ; the representative is a independent commercial agent authorised under the authority of a network.
  • The agent bears his or her expenses, chooses his or her organization and has no fixed salary or fixed hours.
  • The share of fees paid is structurally higher in a network of agents, but it applies to an activity without guaranteed income.
  • Neither of the two statutes is better in itself: choice depends on the need for security, the availability of cash and the commercial appetite.

Are you considering a career in real estate and you are hesitant between joining a traditional agency or a network of agents like iad France? This comprehensive comparison will help you make the right choice with full knowledge.

The fundamental differences

Real estate agent in agency and independent real estate agent are two different trades with very different modalities, even if the final objective is the same: to support customers in their real estate transactions.

Commissions: a considerable gap

This is often the first argument that makes real estate professionals switch to proxy networks. The difference in compensation is significant.

Concrete example: For a sale of 300 000 euros with a fee of 3% (tax excl. 9 000 euros), an agency agent will receive between 2 700 and 4 050 euros. An iad agent will collect between €6,210 and €7,650 for the same transaction.

Freedom: The Decisive Factor

In the past, freedom is often the driving force behind the transition to the status of agent. At iad France, you choose:

  • Your working hours
  • Your geographical sector
  • Your methods of prospecting
  • Your pace of development
  • The number of assets you manage simultaneously

Training: an iad advantage often undervalued

iad Academy offers an online training platform that is accessible on a continuous basis. Training covers real estate legislation, sales techniques, negotiation, real estate tax and much more. This training also meets the 14h/year obligation imposed by the Alur Act.

Complementary income: the decisive advantage

This is the point where iad radically stands out from the traditional agencies. By developing a team of advisors, you can see management committees on their activities:

  • 7% to 14% on sales of your direct godson (Level 1)
  • 3 % on sales of your godson's godson (level 2)
  • 1.5% at level 3
  • 0.5 % to 1 % at level 4
  • 0.2% to 1% at level 5

iad contract terms and conditions in force, verified by Guillaume Roque in August 2026. These conditions can change: only the iad contract documentation submitted during integration is authentic.

These management commissions depend on the sales actually made and cashed by the advisors of your organization, not on your own sales activity. They are variable, without guaranteed amount or regularity.

Who should choose iad agent status?

The iad representative status is particularly suitable for the following profiles:

  • Real estate agents frustrated by their weak agency commissions
  • Traders in conversion who wish to enhance their skills
  • People seeking freedom and professional independence
  • Those who want to build an organization with heritage value
  • Ambitious profiles that want to develop a team and recurrent income
