Real estate in Béziers en 2026 :
prices, neighbourhoods and opportunities

By Guillaume Roque • Representative iad France — Narbonne • Reading: 6 min •Published on 14 April 2026 • Updated: 14 August 2026

Béziers has experienced a remarkable increase in attractiveness in recent years. With some of the most accessible prices in Languedoc, the city is attracting more and more buyers and investors. Overview of the market of the Dickrois in 2026.

The real estate market in Dickrois in 2026

Attractive prices

Béziers remains one of the most accessible cities in the region. The average price per m² for an apartment is between 1 400€ and 2 000€, and between 1 800€ and 2 800€ for a house. Prices that attract first-time dealers and investors.

A city in transformation

Béziers benefits from major urban renewal projects that gradually enhance the city's heritage. The historic centre, with its private hotels, finds a new attraction among buyers seeking authenticity.

The neighborhoods of Béziers to know

Historic centre and plateau

The heart of Béziers with its cathedral and medieval alleyways. Character goods at still affordable prices — 1,600 to 2,200€/m².

Plateau des Poètes et Gambetta

Popular residential area, close to the famous Poet Garden. Ideal for families with children.

Villeneuve-lès-Béziers and periphery

For houses with garden at very accessible prices. High potential for recovery.

Agde and Cap d'Agde

Outside Béziers but in its area of influence, ideal for seasonal rental investment.

Beziers: an opportunity for investors

Rental investment: what to calculate

No percentage return is reported here: the differences between two neighbouring buildings are too large for an average to make sense. The calculation is done in good form, from the actual purchase price, the rent actually charged on the street concerned, the vacancy rate recorded, the co-ownership charges, the property tax, the works and your personal taxation.

A university city

Béziers has several higher education institutions which provide regular rental demand throughout the year.

Tips for your real estate project in Béziers

Buy in the historic center

This is where the best opportunities for valorisation are found in the medium term. Renovated properties are resold very well.

Attention to DPE

Many ancient properties in Béziers have a bad DPE. Check the cost of energy renovation before purchasing.

Use a local professional

Knowing the retail market is essential to avoid pitfalls and find the best opportunities.

The commune of Béziers, in the Hérault, and its first crown.

What goods are actually found there?

  • Relationship buildings and bourgeois apartments of the late 19th century
  • House in a suburb with courtyard
  • Village villas in the west
  • New programmes in the periphery

Béziers has very marked differences of value over short distances: two buildings separated by 300 meters can be covered by totally different markets. The bourgeois heritage of the centre, which has been undervalued for a long time, is clearly distinguished from the degraded structure of certain suburbs.

Frequently asked questions about Béziers

Why do prices vary so much in Béziers?

Because the city juxtaposes very different micro-markets on a restricted perimeter. A municipal average price therefore makes little sense: only a comparison on the scale of the street and the building is exploitable.

Is an old apartment in the centre a good purchase?

The potential exists, but it is judged on the building: condition of the common parts, voted works, amount of charges, presence of an elevator. These elements weigh heavier than the surface itself.
