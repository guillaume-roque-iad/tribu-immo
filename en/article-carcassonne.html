Real estate in Carcassonne
in 2026

By Guillaume Roque • Manager Platinum iad France • Reading: 6 min •Published on 26 June 2026 • Updated: August 15, 2026

Carcassonne accounts for 2,294 of the housing sales recorded between 2023 and 2025 in the sector followed here, of which almost half are apartments. This page starts from these real sales to describe three markets that coexist in the same municipality: the approaches to the City under heritage constraint, the Bastide and its park of ancient apartments, and the pavilion crown.

Carcassonne real estate market in 2026

What the 2,294 sales recorded say

Of the last three full calendar years, 1,169 home sales and 1,125 apartment sales were recorded in Carcassonne and withheld after filtering. This is the highest volume of the sector after Narbonne, and it allows for an annual calculation.

Who buys in Carcassonne

According to the goods observed by Guillaume Roque on the sector, the buyers come partly from outside the department — the Toulouse basin, other regions, foreign — and compare the budgets with those of their area of origin. This observation of the ground does not allow us to conclude that prices are generally changing: for this reason, we must see registered real estate transactions over a full period.

Carcassonne's particularity is due less to the volume of demand than to its composition: a significant proportion of buyers discover the city through tourism, which shifts their criteria from those of a local buyer — the view and the stamp pass before the surface.

Why buy in Carcassonne

A privileged situation and access

About 50 minutes from Toulouse by train, on the A61 motorway, and with its own airport, Carcassonne enjoys rare connectivity for a city of this size. This accessibility makes it an ideal basis of life for teleworking workers as well as those who radiate between Toulouse, Narbonne and the coast.

A living environment between heritage and nature

Shops, schools, cultural and sports facilities, lively markets: Carcassonne offers all the services of a prefecture while remaining human-sized. The city, the Canal du Midi, the Corbières and the nearby Montagne Noire make up a sought after environment, between heritage, vineyards and large spaces.

Selling in Carcassonne: the heritage constraint first

Whether the property is in a protected area

This is the first check to be made, even before talking about prices. On the outskirts of the City and in part of the Bastide, facades, carpentry and roofs are architectural requirements: works are subject to authorisation, materials are imposed, and delays are extended. A buyer who discovers it after the compromise reopens the negotiation — or renounces.

Announce this constraint as soon as the announcement is made, with the exact perimeter and what it involves. It scares away the rushed buyers, but it reassures those who are precisely looking for this kind of good, and it is they who are buying.

Collect folder before uploading

On old city centre buildings, the buyer wants to know what has already been done: roof repair, electricity, insulation, work. The invoices and town planning authorizations obtained are considered sales arguments. Their absence turns every question into doubt.

Parking, often decisive in the Bastide

According to the property observed by Guillaume Roque on the area, the issue of parking is almost every visit in the heart of the city. A garage, private space or even accessible residential parking changes the perception of an identical surface property. If the property does not have it, it is better to announce and document the existing solutions nearby rather than let the buyer discover it on site.

The Carcassonian apartment market, the most affordable in the area

With a median of 67 500 € for 55 m², the carcassonne apartment displays the lowest price per square metre of all the municipalities followed on this site: 1 253 €/m², compared to 2 222 €/m² in Narbonne. A quarter of the sales even ended in €53 000.

This level can be explained by the nature of the park: a large part of the old condominiums of the Bastide, often without elevator, parking and with a degraded energy classification. For a seller, the consequence is direct: in this segment, each missing item is visibly removed from the price, and the three that weigh the most are parking, elevator and the actual amount of co-ownership charges.

On an old condominium, the last three minutes of general meeting and the status of the proceedings voted are considered sales arguments when they are provided from the outset. Their absence turns every question of the buyer into suspicion, and suspicion is negotiated.

Three price regimes in one municipality

The approaches to the City, under patrimonial constraint

The most framed perimeter: facades, carpentry and roofs are architectural requirements, work is subject to authorization and delays are extended. This constraint selects buyers rather than lowers prices — it removes those who are in a hurry and retains those who are precisely looking for this type of goods.

La Bastide Saint-Louis, a shopping heart in checker

This is where the apartment market is concentrated, and where parking becomes a leading criterion. On the same surface, a property with a garage or a private space does not compare to a property without a parking solution.

The flagatory crown, another market

West and south Aude right bank: land, parking, more recent construction. The median plot of houses sold in the commune, 303 m², mainly reflects this segment. The majority of the purchasers are principal residents, who are sensitive to rail and motorway services rather than to the stamp.

Frequently asked questions on the Carcassian market

Why are the prices per m² announced elsewhere for Carcassonne higher?

Because many sites publish ad prices, i.e. prices charged, and not signed prizes. DVF records what appears in the notarial act. The gap between the two corresponds to the negotiation, which is not marginal on old buildings. The figures on this page are those of sales actually concluded.

Is buying near the city imposing particular constraints?

Yes. The approaches to the City are heritage protections: modifications to the façade, carpentry or roof are subject to authorization and aesthetic requirements. Verification of the applicable perimeter is done in the town hall, before the estimate and not after the compromise.

Is it better to sell a rented or empty Carcassian apartment?

This depends on the energy classification and the current lease. Since 1stir January 2025, a G-class dwelling no longer meets the energy decency criterion and can no longer be subject to a new housing lease (Ministry of Ecological Transition). On a G-rated property, investor customers are severely restricted: selling to an occupant often becomes the shortest route.
