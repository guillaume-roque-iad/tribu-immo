Part-time real estate: how can you plan a realistic week?

Guillaume Roque · Published on 9 November 2026

To start in real estate while keeping a salaried job, begin with your genuine availability. Split it between learning, conversations and follow-up. A sustainable routine beats an overloaded calendar: no schedule guarantees a sale or income.

Check compatibility with your employment

In France, combining salaried employment with self-employment may be possible under certain conditions. Check your contract, any collective agreement and your circumstances: duty of loyalty, competition with the employer, exclusivity clauses and work outside salaried hours. Exceptions and special rules exist. Seek advice relevant to your situation if uncertain.

Calculate your real availability

First account for work, commuting, personal obligations and rest. Illustrative example: if you have six free hours a week, use two 90-minute blocks, one two-hour block and one hour for review. This is neither an iad requirement nor a success threshold.

Give every block a clear purpose

Set aside time for learning, another period for preparing conversations and appointments, and time to follow up on commitments. Allow room for unexpected changes and offer only appointments you can actually keep. Do not promise instant responses when you cannot provide them.

Connect training to practice

iad describes a programme combining online learning, in-person sessions and field support from a mentor. After every module, identify a real-life case to discuss with that person. Before carrying out regulated professional tasks alone, check the authorization and procedures that apply.

Follow up without scattering data

For every project entrusted to you, record the next step, date and agreed commitments in approved tools. Distinguish initial contacts from meetings actually held. Never use your employer’s files or inappropriate tools to store personal data.

Review your progress after four weeks

Compare planned with actual hours, progress in training and commitments fulfilled. If you become exhausted or tasks pile up, slow down before expanding. Commercial outcomes depend on many factors: a calendar cannot predict commissions.

Frequently asked questions

Are six hours a week enough?

That is only a planning example, not an amount that guarantees results. Prioritize progress and keeping your promises.

Do I have to quit my job first?

Not necessarily. In France, this depends on your contract, activity compatibility and chosen business status. Check your individual circumstances.

How do I know if the plan works?

Look at consistency, the quality of conversations and completed actions. Discuss obstacles with your mentor and change one thing at a time.
