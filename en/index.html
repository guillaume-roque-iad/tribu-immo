YOUR QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU LEAVE

Network conversion or change:
the first landmarks.

Where to start a real estate conversion?

Start by examining your budget, availability and knowledge of the sector. Then discuss the trade and the proposed accompaniment before choosing your training and organization. Preparing for conversion →

How to compare two real estate networks?

Compare fees, remuneration, tools actually included and the availability of accompaniment. Ask for up-to-date documents and a demonstration of services, and then compare these with your needs. Compare networks →

How does a first exchange with Guillaume take place?

The first exchange allows you to talk about your situation, your objectives, the costs and the activity on a daily basis. A 45-minute video conference presentation can then be organized, without commitment. Exchange with Guillaume →
