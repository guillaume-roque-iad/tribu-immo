What this page does not say

Out of honesty, three clarifications that avoid misunderstandings:

  • We do not claim to have teams active in each of these countries. A presence of the iad group is not a presence of La Tribu Immo.
  • The conditions are not the same everywhere. Rates of pay, fees, tools and devices are the responsibility of each subsidiary and may differ significantly.
  • There is no "local SFCR". The special register of commercial agents is a French system. Abroad, it is the country's professional regime that applies — sometimes a license, sometimes a registration to an order, sometimes nothing like it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I join iad from an overseas department?

The official iad website does not mention the overseas departments and regions among its locations. So we do not say so. If you are concerned, please contact us: the availability of the network in your territory will be checked with iad before any further action.

Are the commissions the same in all countries?

No, and there's nothing to say. The remuneration scale is subject to the contractual conditions of each subsidiary.

Should we speak French?

The support provided by La Tribu Immo is in French. The working language of the branch of the country in which you work may be different.

A project in France or in a country where iad is located?

Tell us where you plan to practice. We check what is really possible before you take a step — without any promise, and without any commitment on your part.
